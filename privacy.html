Privacy policy

News Watch is used only by its owner, Kevin Bundy. With his permission it uses the YouTube Data API to manage one playlist on his own YouTube account: it adds and removes videos in that playlist and reads the playlist's contents.

It collects no data about anyone else, shares nothing with third parties, and stores its sign in token only on the owner's own computer. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
